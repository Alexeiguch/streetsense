# Pedestrian signals by country

Researched 31 Oct 2026 with web search and fetch. Each cell cites a source key from the **Sources** table at the end. "Unverified" means I found no primary or official source that day; treat it as a guess to check with real footage, not as a fact. Nothing here was measured on our reader.

## Safety principle (read first)

StreetSense only says "go" when the local reader says walk **and** the Muse Spark model agrees. Anything unclear means wait. Country data only tells the reader what to expect. It never authorises a crossing on its own.

## Families at a glance

| Family | Walk | Clearance phase | Stop | Countries |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **NA** hand/person | white walking person | flashing orange hand, orange countdown beside it | steady orange hand | USA, Canada, Argentina (Buenos Aires), Mexico (partly) |
| **FG** flashing green | green walking figure | green figure flashes | red standing figure | Spain, Portugal, Poland, Netherlands, Austria, Switzerland, Nordics, Chile, Japan, South Korea, Hong Kong, Singapore, UAE, UK/Ireland pelican crossings |
| **FR** flashing red | green walking figure | red figure flashes | steady red figure | Brazil, Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, India |
| **AM** amber pedestrian | green figure | steady amber aspect (3-lamp head) | red figure | Italy, Ireland (junctions), China; reported for Germany and Central Europe |
| **NF** no flash | green figure | green goes dark or red comes on at once | red figure | UK puffin and junctions, France, Germany (StVO lists only red and green) |

A country can show more than one family (UK pelican vs puffin, Mexico by city). Countdown displays are an add-on that crosses families, see the column below.

## Implications for StreetSense

These points are **inferred from the notes in READER.md and the tables above, not measured.**

1. **Brazil (FR) is our clearest gap.** Brazilian heads flash the *red* figure during clearance [S4]. Our reader expects a flashing green figure or a hand. A blinking red lamp next to a dark green lamp, plus the square focus, plausibly triggers the countdown guard or reads as "other". This fits the "read as countdown" symptom. Any "Brazil countdown during green" is unverified.
2. **Countdown-during-walk countries.** The `figure_walk_guard` turns walk plus digits into non-walk (READER.md). That is safe, but it will never say walk at crossings where the count runs during green: South Korea [S37], Hong Kong trials [S17], Japan remaining-time [S35], China [S1], Singapore [S40]. Safe failure, but useless there.
3. **Amber family (AM).** Italy, Ireland and China have a third, amber pedestrian lamp [S30][S27][S1]. The reader has no pedestrian amber state. It must map to wait.
4. **Night and green figures.** The night bloom rule needs a green halo. Japan's blue-green [S1] and Hong Kong's flashing green at night are likely to fall below it. night_jp already has no detection (READER.md).
5. **No-flash UK puffin and France** show a red figure straight after green [S6]. This is the easiest family for us. The near-side puffin unit is small and low, so detection range is the risk, not the logic.

Families the reader likely fails on today: **FR** (Brazil first), **AM**, countdown-in-walk sites of **FG** (Asia), and night **FG**. **NA** and daytime **FG/NF** in Europe are the ones we tune on.

### Family reference data and runtime profiles

`data/signal_families.json` maps ISO country codes to a family plus flags and remains a documentation reference. The [UK/European reader profile](SIGNAL_PROFILES.md) now uses an explicit country hint to select symbol-style checks and bounded local head refinement. It does not consume this file's phase policies. The style hint can only restrict an existing WALK; refinement supplies additional current-image detections to the unchanged reader and confirmation rules.

```json
{
  "families": {
    "NA": {"walk": "white_person", "stop": "orange_hand", "clear": "flash_hand", "countdown": "clearance_only"},
    "FG": {"walk": "green_figure", "stop": "red_figure", "clear": "flash_green"},
    "FR": {"walk": "green_figure", "stop": "red_figure", "clear": "flash_red"},
    "AM": {"walk": "green_figure", "stop": "red_figure", "clear": "amber"},
    "NF": {"walk": "green_figure", "stop": "red_figure", "clear": "none_or_dark"}
  },
  "countries": {"US": {"family": "NA"}, "BR": {"family": "FR", "verified": true}}
}
```

Rule if this is ever used: a family can only make the reader **more** cautious (for example, treat flashing red as stop in FR). It must never turn a not-walk into walk. Unknown country means use all families and wait when unsure.
